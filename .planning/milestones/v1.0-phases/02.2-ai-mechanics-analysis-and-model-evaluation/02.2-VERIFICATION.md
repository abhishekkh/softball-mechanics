## Summary of Phase 2.2 Goal Achievement

The phase goal was: **evaluate MediaPipe on real softball video, add motion-type tagging to upload flow, add new motion-specific mechanics flags (Bat Casting for hitting, Premature Shoulder Opening for pitching), prototype Gemini Flash VLM as a second opinion layer, and document evaluation findings.**

All five objectives are demonstrably achieved in the codebase:

1. **Motion-type tagging in upload flow** — Hitting/Pitching radio in `UploadPageClient`, wired through `VideoUploader` prop into presign fetch body, Zod-validated on the server, written to `videos.motion_type` column.
